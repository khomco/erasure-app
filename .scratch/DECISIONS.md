| D18 | **Returning credits when a station is released** | An owner releases a station. Today the hub puts the station's last *self-reported* balance back in the pool (`credits.rs:346`), and the station keeps its signed grants. A customer who edits that report, or simply keeps the released station running offline, can spend the same credits twice. (a) return nothing until the station hands its grants back over the signed link (air-gapped release: credits are lost, or returned by vendor support); (b) keep returning the reported balance and rely on the mismatch flag; (c) never return credits on release. *Recommended (a). Until decided: (b), today's behaviour, with the hole open.* | Closing the credit-minting hole (hardening assessment) |
| D19 | **Recovering a station whose clock ran ahead** | A bench's clock was set to 2031 by mistake, then corrected. The station's watermark says it has seen 2031, so under ADR-0013 it refuses every real start as a clock rollback, and "correct the clock" cannot help. Today the only way out is deleting its lease file, which also throws away its credit reserve. (a) a signed reset: the hub issues one over the ADR-0008 link to a connected station, and a vendor admin issues a reset file for an air-gapped one; (b) cap the watermark at the licence's `not_after` plus a margin, so the jump can never exceed the licence; (c) leave it as is. *Recommended (a). Until decided: (c).* | Bench recovery without losing credits (deferred O-17) |
| D20 | **Keeping signed certificates on the station** | Signed certificates and Jobs live only in the station's memory (CONTEXT §6 #2: evidence in RAM, certificates streamed off). Until decided: (b).* | Crash-safe certification before real hardware (hardening assessment) |
