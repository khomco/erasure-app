| D15 | **Annual offerings through Stripe: one charge or a subscription** (offerings-and-payments story 3, decision 1) | *About:* an organization owner buying an annual offering in the customer portal; checkout sends them to Stripe Checkout. Today the hub charges the year once (Stripe `mode=payment`) and nothing renews. *Problem:* a Stripe subscription would charge the card again next year automatically, but the hub cannot yet grant a renewed year (fulfilment and renewal are story 4), so a customer could be charged for something they never receive. *Options:* (A) one charge now, with story 4 choosing the renewal mechanism: the customer pays once and must buy again next year; no risk of an unfulfilled charge; rules out automatic renewal until story 4. (B) a Stripe subscription now: renewals charge automatically; costs a second event path (`invoice.paid`) and the risk of charging without fulfilment; rules out a non-Stripe renewal design. *Recommendation:* A, because it never takes money the hub cannot yet honour, and story 4 can still choose subscriptions. *Default:* A ships. Should annual offerings be charged once until story 4 (A) or as Stripe subscriptions now (B)? | Story 4's renewal design |
| D16 | **Stripe invoices** (offerings-and-payments story 3, decision 2) | *About:* customers who need an invoice for a purchase; the portal's Billing button opens Stripe's billing portal, which shows saved cards and payment history, and Stripe emails a receipt after each payment. Today no invoice document is produced. *Problem:* for one-off Checkout payments Stripe issues post-payment invoices only as a paid add-on (a per-invoice fee), so turning them on is a cost choice; a buyer whose finance team needs a formal invoice gets only a receipt. *Options:* (A) leave invoices off until story 4's renewal mechanism: no extra Stripe fee; buyers get receipts only; CAP-5's "invoices" stays a residual. (B) turn on Checkout's `invoice_creation` now: buyers get a numbered invoice; costs Stripe's invoicing fee on every sale and a small code change; nothing ruled out. *Recommendation:* A, because the fee is an owner's cost decision and story 4 may bring invoices with subscriptions anyway. *Default:* A ships. Should Stripe invoices stay off until story 4 (A) or be turned on now (B)? | CAP-5 "invoices" |
